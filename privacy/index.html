Privacy notice

Last updated 5 November 2026

Who we are

Re. LLC (“Re.”, “we”) is a hospitality consultancy registered in Masdar City Free Zone, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. This notice explains how we handle personal information collected through re-consultancy.me.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you type into the form: your name, work email, and, if you choose to give them, your phone or WhatsApp number, company or venue, and a short message about your business.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this website.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, arrange a conversation or demo, and keep a record of our correspondence with you. We do not sell your information or share it for marketing.

Who processes it

Enquiry forms are delivered to us by Formspree, a form-handling service. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process data on our behalf and may store it outside the UAE.

Re. Planner and Re. Academy accounts at app.re-consultancy.me are covered by the terms agreed with each client.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the length of the relationship and any period required by law.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing hello@re-consultancy.me. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
