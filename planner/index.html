Why it exists

Most venues see their P&L a month late. By then the month is gone. The Planner shows it every week, built to the operator's week, not the accountant's year.

Two tools

From the first plan to every week of trading.

01

Business plan

For a new opening or a project

  • ·Concept, seats and opening month

    Start from the venue you have in mind.

  • ·Sales by season

    Covers times spend per head, set for high, normal and low months.

  • ·Costs, team and rent

    Cost of sales, the roster, rent and running costs.

  • ·Investment and payback

    Fit-out, funding, ROI, five years and cash flow, if you need them.

When the venue opens, the plan becomes its budget.

Questions

Does it replace our accountant?

No. Your accounts stay with your accountant. The Planner is for running the venue: it works on the operator's week, so you can act before the month closes.

Can we use it before we open?

Yes. Build the business plan first. When you open, it becomes the venue's budget, so nothing is entered twice.

One venue or a whole group?

Both. Each venue enters its own numbers. Owners see every venue side by side, and managers only see their own.

Who can see our numbers?

Only the people you give access to. Your financials are covered by an NDA and are never shared or compared with other clients.

Do we get support from Re.?

Yes. We set it up with you, and on a Re. engagement we review the numbers with you every week.
