The journey

From concept to P&L, in six steps.

01 · Business plan

Start from the venue you have in mind.

Concept, seats and opening month; sales by meal period, day and season; costs, team and rent; investment, ROI, payback and shareholders. Export to PDF, Excel or Word, or fill the client input workbook.

02 · Forecast · 03 · Budget

When you open, the plan becomes the budget.

Nothing is entered twice. Import your own budget workbook, or build it with us, and update the forecast as the year moves.

04 · Weekly P&L

Each venue enters its own week.

Sales, covers, costs and manning, once a week. Weeks roll up into the month, against budget, forecast and last year.

05 · Business health

One score out of 100, six pillars.

Graded A Excellent, B Healthy, C Watch, D At risk, against targets you set per venue.

Profitability & cost 30%Commercial 25%Guest 15%Operations 12.5%Menu 10%People 7.5%

06 · Action plan

What to fix next, and who owns it.

Actions from a library of proven fixes, ranked by the health points each wins back, each with an owner such as the GM or Executive Chef.

Questions

Planner, plainly.

Can we use it before we open?

Yes. Build the business plan first; on opening it becomes the venue's budget.

One venue or a whole group?

Both. Managers see their own venues; owners see every venue side by side.

Who can see our numbers?

Only the people you invite. Your financials are kept confidential and never shared or compared with other clients.

Does it replace our accountant or POS?

No. Your accounts stay with your accountant. Managers enter the week's results; POS and inventory links are planned.

How is it priced?

Per venue, monthly or annually, on its own or together with Re. Academy. Prices are shared in the demo meeting.
