The best restaurants we've worked in were never the ones with the most ideas. They were the ones willing to look at a dish, a shift or a price list that had "always been that way" and ask the uncomfortable question: would we do this again?

Rethink is the first of three things Re. does. Before a single recipe is rewritten, we sit in the dining room, stand at the pass and read the numbers — then we question everything that doesn't earn its place.

Over the next ten weeks we'll share how. Slide through for where it starts.

If there's something in your venue you've stopped questioning, DM us. We'll tell you what we'd do again.

A kitchen doesn't get fixed with a memo.

Rebuild is the second thing Re. does — the part where the new menu meets the pass at 8 pm on a Thursday. Workflow, standards, procurement, rosters, and the training that makes them hold once we've gone home.

Nothing here is glamorous. All of it shows up on the plate and on the P&L.
