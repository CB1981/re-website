Questions

Can I see a training first?

Yes. Hospitality Principles is free to watch: eight minutes, no sign-up.

Can we run our own classroom training on it?

Yes. Schedule your sessions, invite the team and record attendance next to their online trainings and certificates.

Do staff need a laptop?

No. Trainings play on any phone, with voice.

Is it in Arabic?

Yes. Trainings are available in English and Arabic.

Can we choose the trainings?

Yes. You pick the sections for each venue, and managers assign trainings by role or by person.

Do certificates expire?

Food safety and similar certificates carry an expiry date. Managers get a reminder before one runs out.
